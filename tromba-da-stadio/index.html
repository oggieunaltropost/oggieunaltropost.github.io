Stadium Air Horn

A classic air horn, a vuvuzela and a truck horn. Three sounds for cheering from your Android phone.

Google Play

Come si usa

Scegli la tromba e tocca la foto o il pulsante Suona. Tocca di nuovo la foto o premi Stop per fermarla. L’app ricorda la lingua e la tromba selezionate.

How to use it

Pick a horn and tap its photo or the Play button. Tap the photo again or press Stop to silence it. The app remembers your language and horn choice.
